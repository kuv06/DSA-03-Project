Subset DP — Optimal Partition (Sum over Subsets)

SOS, O(n·2ⁿ)

Sum over Subsets precomputes, for every subset of the customer cities, the total depot-distance of that subset — in O(n·2ⁿ) instead of re-adding it up from scratch each time. Those precomputed sums make it cheap to then search, by DP over subsets, for the way to split customers into vehicles that minimizes total distance exactly — unlike the min-cost max-flow panel, this isn't limited to evenly-sized groups.
